Conference review

Choose an officer to review their work and give directions.

No officer matches that name.

Columns follow the width of the page area: five on a wide desktop, then four, three and two; one on a phone.

Each tile: the officer's photo or the initials of their name, the name with the rank in lighter text, and their work in the wording the page uses today, which wraps to a second line when the tile is narrow.

A coloured mark appears only when something needs attention. Most tiles have none.

Order is alphabetical by name, so an officer is always in the same place. Typing in the find box narrows the list; Enter opens the first match.

Test accounts are not listed.
