Every part can be changed

Click the heading, a line, the logo box or the band. Move, resize, turn, change font and colour, hide, lock or delete, the same as in any collage.

There is no default logo on a poster. The logo box shows Add logo until you pick one or choose No logo; with no logo the heading moves up to fill the space.
