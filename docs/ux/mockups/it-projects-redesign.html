Assumptions in this mockup. The time figure uses, in order: (1) the recorded completion date of the stage just before the current one, giving days at the stage with ageing colour; (2) if that stage has no date, the day the system recorded the move, shown light and never coloured; (3) otherwise "date not recorded". An earlier stage's date is never borrowed. A case with no stage done counts from the day it was added. To show the fallbacks, the mockup drops the PPP date of "Video conferencing" and the Bid date of "Laptops for faculty". Ageing bands (owner decision, 8 Nov 2026): up to 30 days normal, 31–60 amber, over 60 red, the same for every stage. Data is the local demo set as of 8 Nov 2026, with the "Demo ·" prefix left off.
