About this mockup

Everything you can click here works. What changes in the real module:

  • Photos are drawn placeholders. You can add your own with Photos → Add photos, or paste one with Ctrl+V.
  • Stickers: hundreds of bundled, open-licensed stickers and emoji that look the same on every computer. The mockup uses this computer's emoji.
  • Fonts: about 20 bundled open fonts, including Hindi fonts (handwritten and formal). The mockup borrows fonts already on Windows.
  • Textures: real paper, kraft, linen and wood pictures shipped with PRISM.
  • Drafts are saved in this browser, with the photos, and can be reopened after a restart. The mockup saves the layout only.
  • Saving makes a real PNG, JPEG or PDF at print quality. The mockup only shows a message.
  • Also coming: selecting several items to move or group them, and copy-paste between collages.
  • Nothing goes to the internet. Nothing is sent to the PRISM server.
